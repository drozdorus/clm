Most "AI-native" teams aren't building an edge — they're running an expensive news feed.

Our founder, Ruslan Drozdov, wrote about it for **AI Horizon Journal**, published by the AI Ethics and Integrity International Association (AIEI). The piece — *The FOMO Tax: How AI Tool-Chasing Became the New Productivity Trap* — draws on how we actually run AI at Calma: not by chasing every release, but by measuring what's already working in the stack before adding anything new.
